Let s=x+y, p=xy, and A_n=x^n+y^n.  The power sum recurrence
A_n=s*A_(n-1)-p*A_(n-2), together with A_2=4A_1, gives
p=(s^2-4s)/2.  Replaying the recurrence makes the second hypothesis

A_4-2A_3=-(s^2/2)(s^2-10s+8)=0.

The frozen domain says s is a denominator, so s is nonzero.  Thus
s^2-10s+8=0 and s=5+sqrt(17) or s=5-sqrt(17).  The other denominators
are also nonzero: their conjugate norms are 32 for s-12 and -64 for
5s-44 (while Norm(s)=8).

Exact recurrence evaluation on both branches gives the target quotient
r=10+2sqrt(17) and r=10-2sqrt(17), equivalently r^2-20r+32=0.  Both
branches are achievable: take x and y to be the two complex roots of
T^2-sT+p for p=11+3sqrt(17) or p=11-3sqrt(17), respectively.
