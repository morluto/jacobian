# Research-status evidence audit

Hard research-methodology benchmark derived from four pinned ResearchMath-14k records. The task separates a dataset's reported theorem status from what its cited evidence actually establishes.

The clean-room verifier checks citation selection, publication chronology, claim scope, and unsupported status inferences. It does not decide the present truth of any open problem or browse the literature.
