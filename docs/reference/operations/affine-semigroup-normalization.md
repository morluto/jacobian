# Two-dimensional affine-semigroup normalization

`affine_semigroup.normalization.compute` returns the minimal generators of
`cone(S) ∩ gp(S)` for a positive, full-rank affine semigroup `S` in `Z^2`.
The result retains the source semigroup and expresses every normalization
generator on its ambient row axes.

The group lattice matters. The operation computes a canonical column-Hermite
basis for `gp(S)`, expresses the input generators in that lattice basis,
computes the two-dimensional cone Hilbert basis there, and maps the result back
to `Z^2`. Thus a semigroup generated by `(2,0)` and `(0,2)` normalizes to those
same two vectors, canonically ordered as `(0,2)`, `(2,0)`: its group is
`2Z^2`, not `Z^2`. A semigroup generated by `(1,0)`, `(1,2)`, `(2,1)` has
group `Z^2` and normalizes to `(1,0)`, `(1,1)`, `(1,2)`.

This is the standard affine-monoid normalization `overline(S) = cone(S) ∩
gp(S)`; Gordan's lemma ensures this intersection is again a finitely generated
affine monoid. See the [Normaliz affine-monoid reference](https://www.normaliz.uni-osnabrueck.de/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Normaliz3.2.0Documentation.pdf),
Appendix A.4, Definitions 6 and Theorem 7. Jacobian uses its own exact bounded
two-dimensional kernel and does not invoke Normaliz.

## Admission

The current envelope requires two ambient rows, a full-rank generated lattice,
and a positive semigroup. The exact cone Hilbert determinant in `gp(S)` is at
most 1,000, and transformed generator coordinates are limited to eight decimal
digits. Matrix/Hermite intermediates and transported result coordinates are
digit-bounded before the finite Hilbert parallelogram enumeration. Exceeding a
bound is an operational admission error, never a partial normalization or a
normality conclusion.
