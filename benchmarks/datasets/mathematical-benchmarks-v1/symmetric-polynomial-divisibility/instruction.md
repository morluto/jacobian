# Exact divisibility certificate

Let `a,b,c,d` be integers and `n>0`. Assume

`n | (a+b+c+d)` and `n | (a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2)`.

Produce an exact sparse-polynomial certificate with integer coefficients showing that

`a^4+b^4+c^4+d^4+4abcd`

belongs to the ideal generated by the two hypothesis polynomials. Submit one multiplier for each generator. Every polynomial is a list of canonical nonzero terms, sorted lexicographically by its four-entry exponent vector; coefficients are canonical integer strings. Each multiplier has total degree at most 3. The verifier accepts only integral coefficients because the divisibility conclusion is over the integers.
