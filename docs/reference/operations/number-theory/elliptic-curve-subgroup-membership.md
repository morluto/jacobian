# Finite-field elliptic-curve subgroup membership

[Documentation home](../../../index.md) · [Tool surface](../../tools.md) · [Operation references](../index.md) · [This domain](index.md)

`elliptic_curve.finite_field.point.membership_in_generated_subgroup.decide`
decides whether a candidate lies in the subgroup generated by a finite tuple
of points on the same exact finite-field elliptic curve. The tuple may be
empty; it then generates the identity subgroup. Each point retains the exact
curve and field presentation.

The operation explores the finite Cayley graph from the identity using the
supplied generators. In a finite group, nonnegative products of generators
contain their inverses, so this closure equals the subgroup they generate. A
found candidate establishes membership; a negative result follows only after
the reachable closure is exhausted.

The Hasse upper bound must be at most 4,096, at most eight generators are
accepted, and the conservative point-addition work and the result's retained
entry envelope are admitted before closure expansion. Resource rejection does not imply
nonmembership.
