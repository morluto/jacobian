factorization certificate as the factor's ascending binary coefficients and
its multiplicity, the actual integer discriminant, and a complete `S4`
cycle-type table with class size, fixed-point count, and whether the class
contributes to the root-mod-p density. Compute the corrected fixed-point
proportion and encoded answer.
