# Declared graph-symmetry orbits

[Documentation home](../index.md)

- Producer: `graph.symmetry.generator_orbits.compute`
- Verification: `graph.symmetry.generator_orbits.verify`
- Producer backend: Jacobian standard-library finite orbit closure
- Checker: isolated standard-library permutation, adjacency, color, and orbit replay

The producer computes one atomic outcome: the exact vertex and edge orbit
partitions under the subgroup generated by explicitly supplied
color-preserving automorphisms of one finite labelled simple undirected graph.

## Bounded contract

The request reuses `SimpleUndirectedGraph` and accepts at most 256 vertices,
4,096 edges, 64 declared generators, and 64 characters per vertex or generator
label. These limits keep every valid request, result partition, and checker
replay below the artifact budget. Every generator is a total vertex
permutation. Optional vertex and edge colors, when present, bind every object
in the graph's declared order. The request validator checks the complete
permutation, edge set, and color relations before computation or artifact
writes.

The maximum-size regression with 64-character labels, 4,096 edges, 64 identity
generators, and complete 128-character colors serializes to 3,994,778 input
bytes and 1,943,626 result bytes (5,938,404 combined), leaving headroom below
the 10 MiB artifact and checker-input boundary.

The producer closes the declared actions on the finite vertex and edge
carriers. Every orbit has a canonical index, representative, and complete
member list. The result states `DECLARED_GENERATED_SUBGROUP`,
`COMPLETE_FOR_DECLARED_GENERATORS`, and
`FULL_AUTOMORPHISM_GROUP_NOT_CLAIMED` separately.

The producer remains `COMPUTED`. The operator-authorized checker imports no
producer or graph-symmetry contract implementation. It independently parses
the graph, validates every permutation and preserved relation, reconstructs
both partitions, and binds the exact stored request and result before returning
`VERIFIED`.

## Mathematical and portfolio boundary

An orbit is relative to a specified group action; the
[GAP reference manual](https://docs.gap-system.org/doc/ref/manual.pdf) treats
group actions and their orbits as distinct first-class operations. Maintained
graph libraries can search for mappings and graph symmetries—for example,
[NetworkX graph isomorphism](https://networkx.org/documentation/stable/reference/algorithms/isomorphism.html)—but this capability does not invoke a
search backend or claim that the supplied generators generate the full
automorphism group.

The existing direct graph-isomorphism verifier checks one mapping between two
stored graphs. This capability instead validates zero or more automorphisms of
one graph and computes the complete partitions induced by their generated
subgroup. Generic plugin canonicalization metadata remains untrusted and is not
used as checker authority.

## Reliability boundary

Vertex colors can distinguish terminal classes and edge colors can bind equal
probability classes. The resulting edge orbits are useful, inspectable
compression metadata, but they are not edge-state orbits and do not establish
a reliability identity. This capability does not emit a quotient graph,
canonical label, group order, stabilizer, Burnside count, state-orbit mass, or
terminal connection probability.

In particular, the planar 7,222-vertex counterexample in
[The bunkbed conjecture is false](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02545) remains
answer-visible motivation rather than an acceptance case. Certifying that
example would still require an exact compressed graph construction, an action
lifted to percolation states, invariant event and probability bindings, and a
checker for the resulting weighted orbit sum.

## Development handoff

### Discovery and implementation

- Decision: accept exact orbits under declared color-preserving generators;
  reject full-group discovery and reliability-equivalence claims.
- Portfolio delta: one-graph subgroup action partitions, distinct from the
  existing two-graph explicit-isomorphism mapping verifier.
- Base revision: `851414bad7fffab9afa7631ab95b7691ba8aabf3`.
- Runtime: CPython 3.12 and Jacobian standard library; no dependency change.
- Public reproduction:
  [`graph_symmetry_orbits_public.json`](../../benchmarks/reproduction_cases/graph_symmetry_orbits_public.json).

### Checker and evaluation

- Exact claim: the reported partitions are exactly the orbits of every graph
  vertex and edge under the subgroup generated by the stored mappings.
- Independence: clean-process checker with no producer, contract, NetworkX, or
  proposal/search import.
- Attacks: non-bijections, changed adjacency, broken colors, omitted members,
  split or merged orbits, changed metadata, or rebound payloads must not produce
  `VERIFIED`.
- Public cases are answer-visible and unscored.
- The held-out control/treatment protocol is frozen as `READY_NOT_RUN`; no
  model, prompt, raw trace, or portfolio-improvement claim exists.
- Primary metrics are valid orbit correctness, pre-write invalid-generator
  rejection, full-group overclaim rate, and false `VERIFIED` rate.
- Next action: separately discover an exact compressed graph construction or a
  probability-state group action; neither follows automatically from this
  partition.
